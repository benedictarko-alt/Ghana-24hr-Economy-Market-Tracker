# National discovery audit — Phase Three (Version 22)

**Desk review: 1 November 2026.** This phase focuses on reconciliation and consequential changes, not simply increasing counts.

## Main findings

1. **Agona Amenfi (Western):** Ghana News Agency, 28 October, reports approximately **15% completion**, TEK Constructions and a ministerial warning about slow progress. Reported percentage is not an independent site measurement.
2. **Adum-Banso / Mpohor (Western):** The Western Regional Minister reports a contract termination at the **Mpohor** 24-hour market. Mpohor is a district label, whereas the Tracker already includes the Adum-Banso project within Mpohor-Fiase. **Do not add a new Mpohor record until project identity is checked.** Re-award not independently documented.
3. **Ahanta West and Tarkwa-Nsuaem (Western):** A 26 October report identifies unresolved land acquisition as a barrier; no evidence for an active construction stage.
4. **Agbogbloshie (Greater Accra):** Accra Metropolitan Assembly, 23 September, states the proposed redevelopment was **put on hold for stakeholder consultations**. This replaces an outdated simple 'proposed/relocation' label.
5. **Akatsi (Volta):** A 27 October GNA report identifies a proposed **GHS31m** market budget item for 2027. The existing market entry must not be duplicated: clarify whether the proposal concerns the same project or an additional phase. No inference of expenditure.
6. **Bono East:** RCC identifies **11 projects** but names ten places and 'other beneficiary communities'. The eleventh town cannot yet be established from the published statement.
7. **Ashanti:** Reported 42 projects is a regional aggregate, not 42 independently identified locations. Tebeso No. 2 (55%) and Aboabo (20%) are already in the register.

## Count integrity

**35 audited + 11 separate candidate leads = 46 entries, unchanged.** No distinct new project was established beyond the existing 46 in this batch. 'Mpohor' should not be counted separately from Adum-Banso on current evidence.
