# Nkawkaw market — programme-linkage research note (Version 19)

**Place:** Nkawkaw, Kwahu West Municipality, Eastern Region.

**Central Market redevelopment:** Kwahu West Municipal Assembly reported construction in April 2026 and an eight-month contractor target. Debagio Company Limited was identified as contractor. A July 2026 inspection reported progress, but no independently verified overall completion percentage is established.

**24-hour model market reference:** The 2026–2029 municipal development plan lists a “Design and Construct 1No 24-hour model Economy Market” project in Nkawkaw with a reported GH₵6 million cost. Whether that entry refers to the Central Market redevelopment is **not established**. Do not double-count or add the redevelopment to confirmed 24-hour market totals without clarification.

**Livelihoods:** News accounts describe relocation to temporary markets, muddy conditions, insufficient trading space, customer-access difficulties, reduced patronage and enforcement action against trading around the former market. These are attributed reports, not quantified impact estimates.
