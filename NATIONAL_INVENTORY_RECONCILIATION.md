# National inventory reconciliation — 30 October 2026

This release retains the 12 audited prototype records, and integrates the previous 20-location candidate queue plus Lawra, Gbetsile and Akatsi as **23 separate candidate locations**. They are searchable and charted by region but are **not** added to audited totals. The dashboard shows all 16 regions including those with zero documented records.

## Verification caveats
- Candidate records are provisional and not verified distinct government projects. No new site coordinates were invented.
- Reports of physical works are labelled attributed and not independently corroborated.
- The Bono East RCC report names ten sites although it says eleven projects were inspected. The unnamed eleventh is not invented as a location.
- Sakogu, Langbinsi and Gbintri may refer to overlapping or contested project sites; unresolved.
- Lawra, Gbetsile and Akatsi were reported previously; they are retrospective reconciliation entries, not new government announcements. Source URLs for these three were not available in the underlying working package and are intentionally blank pending re-verification.
- National coverage counts are evidence coverage, not programme performance or nationally verified construction counts.

## Files
- markets.csv/json: existing 12 audited prototype records, unchanged.
- candidate_markets.csv: original simple candidate queue expanded to 23.
- candidate_register.csv/json: normalized 23-location register with review flags and partial source metadata.
- national.js: dashboard regional coverage and candidate directory.
