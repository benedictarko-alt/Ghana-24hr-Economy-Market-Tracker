# Version 13 — Header and data-led infographic carousel

- Headline removed from hero and moved below site name, replacing the justice/accountability tagline. Ghana flag is inline.
- Removed the horizontal mark under the stylised Epa-inspired fallback emblem. This is not a certified reproduction of the traditional Epa symbol.
- Carousel now occupies the former hero area, immediately below header. Six SVG slides are embedded inline to avoid missing assets.
- Slides cover: audited regional distribution, reported stages, selected reported completion percentages, livelihood disruption concerns, documented positive implementation signals (not testimonials), and verification caveats.
- Counts are drawn from markets.csv (35 entries). Percentages are explicitly attributed reports and must not be construed as independently measured progress.
- On very small mobile screens the graphic may be small, and device-specific viewport constraints may still require scrolling.
- Live GitHub site is not modified by this ZIP.
