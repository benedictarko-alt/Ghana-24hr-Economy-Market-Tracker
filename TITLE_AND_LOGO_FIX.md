# Title and logo fix — 30 October 2026

- Site name: The Ghana 24hr Economy Markets Tracker
- Main heading: Tracking the implementation of Ghana’s 24-hour Economy Markets (District Economy Markets) programme
- The logo previously relied on an external Wikimedia Commons image, which may fail to load. The logo now attempts the Wikimedia Epa image, and if that fails displays the bundled `assets/epa-fallback.svg` instead. The fallback is a **stylised handcuff emblem**, not a faithful rendering of traditional Epa.
- The original symbol is attributed to Bosomba Amosah, cropped by Pablo Busatto, CC BY-SA 4.0; see https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Epa_(Adinkra).png.
- Dashboard data, photo placement and navigation are unchanged.
