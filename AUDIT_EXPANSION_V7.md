# Audit expansion V7 — 30 October 2026

Ten Bono East localities promoted from candidate to desk-audited records after checking the Bono East Regional Coordinating Council primary report (published 8 October 2026): Techiman, Nkoranza, Kintampo, Amantin, Yeji, Kajeji, Kwame Danso, Zabrama, Tuobodom, Jema.

**Crucial verification scope:** V1 documents only their inclusion by name in an official regional project review. The RCC states eleven projects inspected, but names ten. It does not provide a project-by-project physical construction stage, MMDA allocation, precise footprint, completion percentage, or operational evidence. Each promoted record is therefore status **Unknown / unverified**, not “Under construction.” The eleventh project remains unresolved; no extra fabricated entry is added.

27 audited records + 8 candidate records = 35 research entries, subject to duplicate reconciliation. None of these totals represent nationwide government construction counts.
