# Audit expansion V8 — 30 October 2026

Six North East Region candidate records were promoted to **desk-reviewed** status using a published interview with the North East Regional Minister (8 October 2026), corroborated for the regional site list by GBC URA Radio's Bunkprugu report (26 October).

- Sakogu, Langbinsi, Nasuan and Chereponi: **Under construction — reported by minister**, V3; not independently inspected/certified.
- Walewale and Yagaba: **Unknown physical construction status**, V3; mobilisation alone does not establish construction.
- Chereponi: a 15 April petition raised *risk* of displacement and sought a site change; it does **not** establish actual relocation or displacement. https://www.citinewsroom.com/2026/04/chereponi-youth-petition-assembly-over-24-hour-market-site/
- Langbinsi / Gbintri: potential naming or siting discrepancy unresolved. No merger or location change made.

**Totals:** 33 desk-reviewed market records, 2 candidate records, 35 research entries (not necessarily distinct projects). Evidence verification of a named claim does not equal site certification.
