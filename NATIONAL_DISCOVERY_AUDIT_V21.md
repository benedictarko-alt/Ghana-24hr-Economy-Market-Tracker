Source: https://gna.org.gh/2025/12/construction-of-24-hour-economy-model-markets-in-upper-east-to-begin-soon-minister/
- **Ahafo:** regional contract-signing announcement without distinct town-by-town site list. Source: https://ahrcc.gov.gh/ahafo-region-signs-contracts-for-24-hour-economy-model-markets/
- **Bono East:** 11 projects reported, but the RCC statement does not clearly identify 11 unique localities; reconcile existing ten records before adding a purported 11th. Source: https://bonoeastrcc.gov.gh/2026/10/bono-east-regional-coordinating-council-reviews-progress-of-24-hour-economy-market-projects/
- **Ashanti:** 42 projects claimed by regional minister; two reported progress figures are Tebeso No. 2 (55%) and Aboabo (20%). These are already in the 35 audited records; **do not double-count**. Source: https://www.isd.gov.gh/ashanti-regional-minister-impressed-with-progress-of-24-hour-economy-model-markets/

## Counts and caveats

- Audited market records: **35**, unchanged.
- Separate candidate leads: **11** (7 from earlier batches, 4 new).
- Combined research entries: **46**, **not** 46 verified distinct projects.
- Candidate entries may overlap with existing projects or represent only budget proposals.
- The October 2026 policy renamed/rescoped the programme as District Economy Markets, with phase one commercial space and phase two amenities; the intended 2028 completion target is a government plan, not an observed completion date. Source: https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/government-to-remodel-24-economy-markets-as-district-economy-markets/
