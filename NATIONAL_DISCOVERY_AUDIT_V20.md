# National identification audit — initial discovery batch (Version 20)

Date: 1 November 2026. This is an **initial targeted documentary sweep**, NOT a completed systematic audit of all 261 MMDAs or all 16 regions.

## Baseline
The original 35 desk-audited records remain unchanged. Nkawkaw remains a separate candidate pending confirmation that its Central Market redevelopment and budgeted 24-hour model market are the same project.

## Other substantial national/regional evidence
- **Ahafo:** Regional Coordinating Council announced signing contracts for model markets across all MMDAs (May 2026); individual sites need to be identified. https://ahrcc.gov.gh/ahafo-region-signs-contracts-for-24-hour-economy-model-markets/
- **Bono East:** RCC's October 2026 statement refers to **11** project sites, while the existing audited register contains **10** Bono East entries. Its text names 10 places and says 'other beneficiary communities'; the eleventh cannot yet be safely identified. https://bonoeastrcc.gov.gh/2026/10/bono-east-regional-coordinating-council-reviews-progress-of-24-hour-economy-market-projects/
- **Ashanti:** Information Services Department reports a regional minister's statement of **42 model markets** across the region. This is an official reported programme-wide figure, **not 42 individually verified locations**. https://www.isd.gov.gh/ashanti-regional-minister-impressed-with-progress-of-24-hour-economy-model-markets/
- **National programme scope:** Government announced October 2026 rebranding to District Economy Markets and two-phase delivery; the intended programme coverage of all 261 assemblies is **not evidence of 261 active sites**. https://gna.org.gh/2026/10/government-to-remodel-24-economy-markets-as-district-economy-markets/

## Verification next steps
1. Reconcile names/aliases and project IDs against the 35 existing records and Nkawkaw candidate.
2. Obtain assembly annual action plans, site-handover certificates, progress reports and contracts for each lead.
3. Search assembly websites in all 16 regions systematically, logging both positive and negative searches and dates.
4. Separate site announcement, physical works, completed structures, operational trading and livelihood disruption.
5. Do not update headline totals, regional shares or infographic statistics until record-level promotion is justified.

**Interpretation:** This batch identifies six candidate locations not present among the 35 audited records; it does not prove six additional operating or under-construction markets.
