NEW RESEARCH LEAD · EASTERN REGION

Nkawkaw Central Market: redevelopment documented, programme link unresolved

Kwahu West Municipal Assembly reported construction of the Nkawkaw Central Market redevelopment in April 2026. Separately, its 2026–2029 development plan lists a 24-hour model Economy Market. It has not been established that these are the same project. Nkawkaw is therefore tracked as a programme-linkage candidate, not counted among the 35 audited 24-hour market records.

Reported livelihood concerns include trader relocation, access difficulties and reduced patronage. See candidate register · Assembly construction report · Municipal development plan.

HOW TO READ THIS

Evidence standards

Proposed / announced

Sod-cutting, handover, award or pledge without verified physical works.

Under construction

Physical works reported or observed; evidence date and attribution retained.

Completed but not operational

Construction completion reported, but trading activity not demonstrated.

Operational

Actual market use confirmed; commissioning alone is insufficient.

Unknown / unverified

Location, stage or attribution lacks adequate evidence.
